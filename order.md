# Place an Order

Thank you for choosing **Fez & Flavores**.

Our products are prepared with care using quality ingredients and are generally made to order. Advance notice helps us confirm availability and provide the best possible quality.

### 1. Explore Our Menu

Visit our Products & Menu page to discover our current selection:

<a class="order-button" href="{{ '/products/' | relative_url }}">

### 2. Prepare Your Order Information

Please include the following information in your message:

- Your full name
- Product or dessert you would like to order
- Quantity required
- Preferred pickup or delivery date
- Preferred pickup or delivery time
- Occasion or event
- Requested flavour
- Requested size or number of servings
- Colours, theme, or decoration requirements
- Pickup or delivery preference
- Any food allergies or dietary concerns

## Customized Cakes and Desserts

Customized orders may include:

- Birthday cakes
- Wedding and engagement cakes
- Anniversary cakes
- Entremets and mousse cakes
- Personalized dessert boxes
- Petite fours
- Moroccan pastry selections
- Ramadan and Eid boxes
- Dessert tables
- Corporate and private-event orders

Customized pricing depends on:

- Product size
- Number of servings
- Flavour and filling
- Ingredients
- Decoration
- Design complexity
- Packaging
- Required preparation time

Please send inspiration photographs when requesting a customized design. Final products will reflect the Fez & Flavores style and may not be identical copies of another artist’s work.

---

## Advance Ordering

Whenever possible, place your order in advance.

More preparation time may be required for:

- Large orders
- Customized cakes
- Wedding and engagement orders
- Dessert tables
- Ramadan and Eid selections
- Corporate events
- Products requiring specialty ingredients

Availability cannot be guaranteed until the order is confirmed.

---

## Pickup and Delivery

Fez & Flavores is based in **Calgary, Alberta**.

Local pickup and available delivery arrangements will be confirmed when your order is accepted.

Some dry Moroccan pastries may be suitable for shipping outside Calgary. Product suitability, shipping cost, delivery time, and destination must be confirmed before payment.

---

## Allergies and Dietary Concerns

Please inform us about allergies and dietary concerns before your order is confirmed.

Some products may contain or come into contact with:

- Wheat and gluten
- Milk and dairy products
- Eggs
- Almonds and other tree nuts
- Peanuts
- Soy
- Sesame

We will explain whether a request can be accommodated, but customers with severe allergies should discuss their requirements carefully before ordering.

---

## Pricing Notice

Prices shown on the blog or menu may change according to:

- Ingredient costs
- Product size
- Quantity
- Customization
- Packaging
- Seasonal availability
- Delivery requirements

The final confirmed price provided for your specific order will apply.
